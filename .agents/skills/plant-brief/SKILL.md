# Plant brief

Output: `models/board/plant/specs/<id>.json`, `"kind": "plant"`, passing `python3 models/shared/tools/check_spec.py <spec>`. Read `models/plant/CONTRACT.md`, `models/STYLE.md` and the archetype's docstring first. Worked example: `models/plant/specs/mermaids_cup.json`.

1. **Inputs** (from season-seeds' `needs` entry): biome id (entry in `models/board/biomes.json`; its `medium` decides wind vs water sway), rarity (common / rare / exotic), habit name, calendar season, `plot` + `gen`; `models/board/roster.json` (no repeated species; `kind: "plant"` entries). Spec carries `plot`, `gen`, `habit`.
2. **Species**: a real plant / alga / fungus with a graphic silhouette per stage (seed → sprout → leafy → bud → bloom). `reference` = binomial + 2–4 traits for the late stages.
   - Biome identity: follow `look.plants`, `look.shape`, `look.palette` and `identity_rules`. Never use a motif another biome `owns`. The bloom (`palette.accent`) carries the biome's accent colour.
3. **Generator: the species gets its own** (`models/plant/archetypes/<id>.py`, spec `archetype` = `<id>`, `based_on` = the growth form it was copied from), like creatures. Pick the growth form closest to the species' real shape, copy it, and build the traits that make the species read (its silhouette at bloom, the leaf shape, the head) into the generator's geometry; parameters alone only resize what the form already draws. Growth forms (`models/plant/archetypes/README.md`, each with an example in `plant/specs/examples/`): `stem_bloom` (one stalk, alternate leaves, one terminal flower head), `blade_clump` (a tuft of arching strap blades, optional seed spike), `blade_fan` (a short stalk with broad fan or paddle blades), `rosette` (a ring of leaves at the soil, optional centre flower stalk or cluster), `spray` (a leafy stalk branching into many small florets: plume, umbel, raceme), `brush` (a stalk topped by a dense tuft or dome of filaments). Nothing close → write a new form per the README and note it for the orchestrator. Only when a form as is already is the species (its own example, say) may the spec name it directly, with `notes.why_library_form` saying why; spec-check refuses anything else (`check_spec.py`, proven on planted specs).
4. **Params**: the fields in the chosen form's docstring (`shape.*`, all blendable numbers), plus `size.height_m` (bloom height, 0.03–3), `shape.stage_heights`, `shape.seed_ratio`; `palette {main [stem, leaf], accent, disc, seed}` (STYLE ranges, checked on `main`); `material {roughness, coat}`; `motion.sway {frames 48–144, cycles whole, amp_deg ~4–7}`, `motion.tend {frames 24–72, lift_deg, open, stretch}`, optional `motion.medium` override; `budget {tris ≤ tier, texture}` (CONTRACT table; 512 px is plenty for forms of many small parts, which keeps the file under its size cap). Rigs stay under 32 bones: share bones between parts (`plant_kit.group`); `plant_kit.rig` refuses more.
5. **Season** (all rarities): the calendar season shapes the plant, never breaking biome identity. Prefer a species that is in its prime that season (winter: hellebore, winter jasmine, snowdrop-like algae; spring: fresh bulbs and buds; summer: full, saturated bloomers; fall: asters, russet grasses, late fungi), adapted to the biome. Then a seasonal finish: frost-tipped leaves, fresh green, sun-bleached petals, russet edges. Note both in `reference`.
6. **Rare seeds are creative.** Rare and exotic plants get one playful **accessory** tied to the habit: a tiny hat, a running-shoe leaf for "Run", a book-page petal for "Read", a drumstick stamen for "Drums". Spec: `accessory {item, why, stage_from (3 or 4), attach: head|stem|leaf}`. Build it in the plant's own generator (`plant/archetypes/<id>.py`, copied from the closest archetype, like creatures), as `s<n>_acc_*` parts from `stage_from` on, inside the bloom's bbox +20%, in the style lock (no text, no logos). The season can theme the accessory too (a knit beanie in winter, a sun hat in summer, a scarf leaf in fall). Exotic may go further: an odd growth form (novel path) as well as the accessory. Common plants: no accessory, a real species.
7. `name`, `lore` (≤ 60 chars; rare ones may nod to the habit). Run check_spec until it passes.
